# Publication figures

Charts use the six published designs and the M9.4 leave-one-design-out endpoint scores.

Corpus: 6 designs, 79856 nodes, 189028 edges, 173 expanded labels.

Easiest holdout by mean defined endpoint F1: aes (0.8333).
Hardest holdout by mean defined endpoint F1: ibex (0.4667).

Undefined F1 values, from holdouts where a model predicted no violations, are left out of that mean.
